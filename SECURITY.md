# Reporting security issues

Report Hyena vulnerabilities to the maintainers of [mitchell-johnson/Hyena](https://github.com/mitchell-johnson/Hyena).

Private vulnerability reporting is not currently enabled on the repository. Open a minimal issue requesting a private reporting channel, without exploit details, credentials, or personal data. Share the affected version, reproduction steps, and impact privately once a maintainer provides that channel.

Hyena is alpha software. Review its [privacy and operational limits](docs/implementation.md#explicit-release-limits) and keep deployment secrets and backups secure.
